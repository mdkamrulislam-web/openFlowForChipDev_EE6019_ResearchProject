# EE6019 risk assessment

**Review draft — 7 October 2026.** Scope: desk and computer work for ASIC design. Likelihoods are provisional estimates before controls. Confirm the local controls and required departmental form with the supervisor; no project-specific form has been supplied.

| Hazard or risk | Who it affects | Likelihood | Impact | Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prolonged screen use; poor posture | Researcher | Medium | Eye strain; neck, back or arm discomfort | Assess chair, screen height and desk layout; use a separate keyboard/screen for prolonged laptop work; take short breaks and change activity. [1] |
| Faulty laptop battery, charger or lead | Researcher; nearby people | Low | Electric shock, burns or fire | Follow charging instructions; inspect equipment; stop using faulty or overheating items; report defects for competent repair. [2] |
| Trailing cables or clutter | Anyone using the workspace | Low | Trips and falls | Route leads away from walking areas; keep floors and exits clear. [2] |
| Fire; unfamiliar escape arrangements | Building occupants | Low | Serious injury | Check the local evacuation plan, exits, assembly point and extinguisher locations; follow alarms and fire marshal instructions. [3] |
| Late or lone working | Researcher | Low | Delayed help after illness or accident | Confirm departmental permission and procedures; prefer staffed periods; agree check-ins and access to emergency help. [4] |
| Workload and December exams | Researcher | Medium | Fatigue; missed milestones | Keep exam weeks light; front-load major runs; review scope and workload with the supervisor. |
| Research template unconfirmed | Researcher; supervisor | High | Rework; delayed integration | Compare candidates now; obtain confirmation before committing the accelerator interface or PDK. |
| Shuttle date changes or unavailable budget | Researcher; supervisor | Medium | Fabrication delayed | Recheck dates/costs before decisions; retain a verified submission-ready layout; seek approval before any paid submission. |
| Tools fail on student machines | Researcher; students | Medium | Teaching or build blocked | Use GitHub Actions; offer the template dev container; test the guide on a clean machine. |
| Tool or PDK version changes | Researcher; students | Medium | Different results; broken builds | Record template revisions and pin tools; upgrade deliberately and rerun verification. |
| Lost work or disk failure | Researcher | Low | Lost progress or evidence | Commit and push at least weekly; keep a separate backup of working files and downloaded run evidence. |
| GitHub outage or Actions limits | Researcher; students | Medium | Builds delayed | Allow buffer before deadlines; retain run artifacts; use the pinned local/dev-container route when available. |

Safety controls follow [HSA workstation guidance][1], [HSA electrical/fire guidance][2], [UCC evacuation guidance][3] and [HSA lone-working guidance][4] (checked 7 October 2026). Ratings and project controls are planning judgements for review. Reassess before adding practical electronics work.
